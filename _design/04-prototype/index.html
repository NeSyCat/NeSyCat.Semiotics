NeSyCat · Prototype · v0.1 · Interactive editor

Click the Spine, pick a value, watch the form change.

Single editor — every Spine button is wired up. Click a category to open its popover, pick a value (rail tile / slider drag / xy field), and the form on the canvas reflects every attribute simultaneously: weight scales the inner polygon, rotation rotates, scale resizes, location translates, number badges, color fills, shape swaps the polygon. The bottom name bar edits the form's centre glyph.
